# UniMondo CMS 2.0 — mandatory Phase 04 item editing

Status: **implemented on the Phase 04 feature branch; production release unapproved; owner Safari review pending**. The owner accepted Phase 02 section navigation, section-level controls and live previews with this explicit deferral. Phase 04 implementation was separately authorized. See [the implementation and verification report](cms-phase-04/README.md); this reference does not grant release authorization.

Phase 04 must provide individual CMS editing controls for every appropriate homepage content card, process step, fact, testimonial and featured item, rather than limiting editors to section headings and descriptions.

At minimum, cover:

- All six Why UniMondo benefit cards: each card’s supported title, description, icon/image and other displayed content.
- Every How It Works process step: its individual heading, description, displayed icon/image and supported position/visibility.
- Each displayed fact and fact card, including Why Study in Europe quick facts and appropriate informational items.
- Individual success stories/testimonials, including their existing published content and related presentation fields.
- Individual destination cards and featured university/program items, reusing their existing canonical records and managers wherever applicable.
- Any other appropriate card or featured content item on the existing homepage; inventory the real rendered items before declaring Phase 04 complete.

Expose the actual effective saved, inherited or default content in every item editor. Preserve current content until the user explicitly saves an authorized change. Reuse existing records, permission keys, authentication, storage and persistence workflows; avoid duplicate content records or a parallel management system. Existing academic, testimonial and operational data must remain intact.

Where technically practical, selecting an item in the live preview should select/open its corresponding editor, with clear selected-item feedback. Provide an accessible keyboard/item-list alternative. Preview selection must not follow public links, submit forms or accidentally change public content.

Acceptance checks must verify item-by-item editing, correct preview/editor correspondence, immediate unsaved preview updates, safe persistence, permission enforcement and desktop/mobile usability. Verify preservation of existing content and operational modules, including Agreements and student applications. Do not describe these item-level capabilities as complete until they are implemented and verified.
